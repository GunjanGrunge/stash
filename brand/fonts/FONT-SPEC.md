# STASH Typography

## Recommended UI family
**Inter**
- UI/body: 400 Regular
- Labels/navigation: 500 Medium
- Buttons: 600 SemiBold
- Strong headings: 700 Bold

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)
Official project: https://github.com/rsms/inter

## Recommended display / marketing companion
**Space Grotesk**
- Marketing/display: 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)
Official project: https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk

## Brand wordmark
The supplied STASH wordmark artwork is exported as logo artwork and should be treated as a brand asset rather than re-typeset in ordinary UI text.

## Font binaries
Font binaries are intentionally not bundled in this package. Install/download them from their official repositories and retain their OFL license files in your application/vendor workflow.
