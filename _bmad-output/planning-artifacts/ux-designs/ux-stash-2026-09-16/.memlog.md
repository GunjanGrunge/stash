topic: STASH Windows desktop client UX
updated: 2026-09-16T11:22
---

- (decision) Private-beta client is Windows-first. The browser app is deferred until after beta testing; do not design it as a beta surface.
- (decision) Approved direction: clean creator utility — calm, dense file-management working surfaces; expressive creative treatment reserved for Home, Stash it, previews, and asset-type accents; light and dark modes are equal first-class modes.
- (event) User supplied a STASH brand-exploration board in chat. It is a reference artifact and must remain unmodified and uncropped; vector brand exports are still required for implementation.
- (decision) The supplied light and dark splash screens use “Stash it. Find it. Use it anywhere.” This is therefore the approved primary in-app tagline.
- (event) Asset inventory received: primary, monochrome, symbol and wordmark SVG/PNG logos; Windows multi-size ICO/PNG icon; splash screens; hero; five empty-state illustrations; ten demo thumbnail pairs; favicon/PWA/Apple/installer/social exports. Font specification names Inter and Space Grotesk, but font binaries are intentionally absent.
- (change) User approved moving the eight supplied asset folders into the canonical brand/ folder. docs/manifest.json paths and UX spine sources were updated; 78 manifest entries resolve and retain their recorded SHA-256 hashes.
- (decision) Mounted-drive compatibility is a first-class product contract for creative tools, not DAWs alone: DAWs, video editors, motion tools, image editors, 3D tools, and other Windows applications must receive normal filesystem semantics. Acceptance includes low-latency handle open, random/range reads and seeks, efficient directory enumeration/stat, read-ahead/cache behavior, and bounded fail-fast behavior under slow or unavailable network; no filesystem I/O may be allowed to stall a creative application indefinitely.
- (decision) STASH desktop client will be open source and GPLv3-compatible. The WinFsp Rust binding may therefore be used, subject to preserving required license notices and verifying distribution obligations before release.
